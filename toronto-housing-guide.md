# Toronto / North York Housing Guide (2026)

## Government Housing Options

### 1. Rent-Geared-to-Income (RGI) — Toronto Community Housing

You pay ~30% of your income in rent.

- Apply via Access to Housing: toronto.ca → Housing Support → RGI
- Wait times are long — treat it as a long-term plan, not an immediate fix. Eligible applicants join the Centralized Waiting List.
- **Eligibility note:** You're ineligible if any household member owes arrears to a social housing provider in Ontario — unless you've signed and are complying with a repayment agreement.

---

### 2. Toronto Community Housing (TCHC) — Affordable Rent Units

Below market rent, not RGI — faster to get.

- TCHC has affordable rental units for singles and families with lower incomes, with rents set at or below average market rent.
- New 1-, 2-, and 3-bedroom units available at **1070 Eastern Ave** (Don Summerville neighbourhood), ready early 2026.
- Contact: **416-981-4771** | RentalOpportunities@torontohousing.ca

---

### 3. Canada-Ontario Housing Benefit (COHB)

Monthly portable rent supplement paid directly to you.

- Apply through the City of Toronto Housing Services.
- **Note:** Toronto's 2026 COHB allocation was cut significantly — from $38M in 2024 to $7.95M in 2026 — availability is very limited.

---

### 4. Housing Help Centre (SHHC) — One-on-One Support

Free help navigating all of the above.

- Works with you one-on-one to find affordable or subsidized housing.
- Helps fill out RGI applications, mediates landlord disputes, and provides financial assistance if you're behind on rent or utilities.
- **North York location:** TNG Community Services, 5231 Yonge St Unit 200 — Thursdays 1–5pm
- **Phone:** 416-285-8070

---

### 5. Toronto Rent Bank — If You're Behind on Rent

- If you're at risk of eviction due to rental arrears, the Toronto Rent Bank can help.
- Also available: **LEAP** (up to $500 for energy bills) and **Emergency Energy Fund** (up to $1,000 one-time grant).

---

## Reality Check

Over 815,000 Ontario households need subsidized housing but aren't receiving it — demand far exceeds supply, and that gap is projected to grow. RGI is a long game.

**Fastest practical moves:**
1. Contact the **Housing Help Centre (SHHC)** immediately.
2. Apply to the **TCHC Affordable Rent program** simultaneously.
