# Project 113: priorities from the site photos

Ranked by what blocks the next trade or carries a safety/liability risk.
Based only on the three photos in this folder; check against the real scope.

1. **Close the envelope on the wall.** Foam is on and taped. Finish the red tape
   at the remaining seams and around the window and door openings, then fix any
   foam holes/screw heads before strapping is covered.
2. **Check the strapping.** Vertical 2x strapping is up; confirm spacing and
   plumb for the cladding and that the window trim frame is fastened and flashed.
3. **Window and door flashing/sealing.** Do the sill and head flashing and
   seal the rough openings before any cladding goes on.
4. **Cladding.** Once 1-3 are signed off.
5. **Fence and railing.** Leaning fence and broken railing beside the walk are a
   trip hazard; temporary fix now, proper repair at the end.
6. **Porta-potty.** Keep it off the walk edge and on a stable base; remove at
   completion.
7. **Sign.** Broken frame and overgrown base; repair or replace before the
   job is finished (it is the contractor's advertising).
8. **Final cleanup.** Garden restoration, debris and the yellow hose.
